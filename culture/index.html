About GRIT Culture

GRIT Culture is a free project by GRIT EDU for every child and young person who wants to explore Mongolia's history, its cultural heritage, and the customs Mongolian people have kept alive and passed down from generation to generation.

History, heritage and custom are an inseparable foundation of a nation's distinct identity and independence, and they play an important part in shaping each person's sense of who they are and where they belong. Yet today it is not uncommon to see young people who know little about their own history and traditions, or who have not yet come to feel their value.

Mongolia is home to internationally recognised heritage such as Karakorum, Erdene Zuu, the Orkhon Valley, Khöshöö Tsaidam and Burkhan Khaldun, and to a rich body of customs handed down through the generations, such as counting the nine nines of winter. Even so, knowledge about them is mostly scattered across academic works and textbooks, and it rarely reaches children and young people in a form that is clear and engaging.

GRIT Culture sets out to change this by bringing these legends, histories and traditions to everyone through picture books, an illustrated map, quizzes and games. Our goal is to help every child come to know their own history and culture, take pride in it, cherish and protect it, and pass it on to the next generation.
