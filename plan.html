GRIT EDU Kharkhorin

The full plan for our next program: a 21-day summer camp for 30 students in Kharkhorin, Uvurkhangai.

The plan in brief

After our first program in Ugiinuur in 2025, GRIT EDU is moving to a residential summer camp in Kharkhorin, Uvurkhangai. It is the next step towards a program that reaches students in every province of Mongolia.

Why Kharkhorin

Before planning the camp we surveyed 113 students in grades 8–12 at schools in Uvurkhangai. They want to study further, but information, advice and English practice rarely reach them.

90%

Rate their knowledge of scholarships as average or lower

94%

Get no regular college or career counselling at school

96%

Have never taken IELTS or TOEFL

86%

Say a program like this is essential in their area

Students named scheduling, cost and transport as the main barriers to taking part. A free residential camp during the summer holidays removes all three.

Three weeks at the camp

Week 1

Foundation

Arrival and orientation, a diagnostic test, core lessons and team building.

Week 2

Growth

Deeper lessons, a first essay draft, debates, a practice test and the start of a project.

Week 3

Launch

A full mock test, a finished essay, a community project and graduation day.

What every student takes home

  • A personal essay draft
  • Scores from at least two mock tests
  • A checklist for university applications
  • A passion project of their own
  • A GRIT EDU certificate of completion
  • A place in the GRIT EDU alumni network, with monthly online mentoring

We will follow up with students three months, six months and one year after the camp, as we did in Ugiinuur. Sponsors and partners receive a full results report after the program.

Funding

The camp is free for every student, so it is funded entirely by sponsors and grants: about 60% from companies, 20% from local government and 20% from other organisations and individuals.

If we raise less than 70% of the budget, we will still run a shorter camp for 15 students over two weeks.

After Kharkhorin

GRIT EDU is not a one-off project. Each program builds on the last, and our graduates come back as mentors for the next group.
