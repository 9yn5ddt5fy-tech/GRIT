Where we work

We work where the distance is greatest — in the soums of central Mongolia, where a student's nearest university is a day's drive away and scholarship information rarely arrives at all.

We started in the community our founder grew up in, and we are expanding outward.

Ugiinuur, Arkhangai · Completed
Our first program: 20 students, four weeks, a team of seven.

Kharkhorin, Uvurkhangai · In preparation
A three-week residential camp for 30 students from three different soums (Arvaikheer, Kharkhorin, Khujirt), currently being planned.
